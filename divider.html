Bookmark Divider

Each divider is a bookmark with no name, so the bookmarks bar shows only its icon: a thin line.

How to use

  • Add: press the button above, or open the Extensions menu (puzzle piece) and click Bookmark Divider. New dividers appear at the far left of the bookmarks bar.
  • Move: drag it along the bookmarks bar to where you want it.
  • Remove: right-click it, then Delete.

Clicking a divider just opens this page. Nothing else happens.
